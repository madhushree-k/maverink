A · Neural networks and memory

Refik Anadol

Melting Memories · Artificial Realities: Coral · Echoes of the Earth

Anadol turns vast datasets into fluid, architectural projections. Melting Memories uses EEG data from people recalling long-term memories, recorded with the Neuroscape Laboratory at UC San Francisco, and runs it through custom machine-learning algorithms. Artificial Realities: Coral is a data sculpture built from around a billion images of coral. I saw Echoes of the Earth at the Serpentine, which draws on visual archives of nature from around the world, and he has a permanent work at the Museum of the Future in Dubai.

The raw data goes through many transformations, and the final aesthetic can feel formulaic. Still, the work opens real conversations about consciousness, computation and memory, and about where machine processing ends and human creativity begins.
