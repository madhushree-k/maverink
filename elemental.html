Elemental

Battle of the Ions: a playing-card game based on the periodic table.

FORMAT
Playing-card game
ROLE
Concept, game design and illustration
WHEN
2019

What if every element on the periodic table had a personality?

Each card turns an element into a character, with its atomic number, atomic weight, electron configuration and physical state, and one fact that sticks. Silicon is a semiconductor most electronics depend on. Beryllium gives aquamarine its light blue hue. Calcium makes up 50 percent of the mass of bones.
